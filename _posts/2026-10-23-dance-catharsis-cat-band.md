Dance CATharsis is a rhythm game about a cat band playing gigs for a cozy pixel village. It's out now on Google Play.

<img src="/assets/dance-gig.jpg" width="320" height="569" alt="A Dance CATharsis gig. Four cats play drums, bass, keys and lead in four lanes, and paw notes fall toward the rings." loading="lazy">

## Four cats, four lanes

Every cat in the band is one instrument and one lane: drums, bass, keys and lead. Paw notes fall toward the rings, and you tap them in time.

Miss a note, and that cat goes quiet until you hit again. You don't just see a mistake on a score counter. You hear the bass drop out of the song, and you hear it come back.

Every cat brings its own groove, so a new cat in the band means a new part in the song.

## Strays and dance-offs

Strays wander in to the edge of town. To bring one into the band, you win it over in a dance-off: the stray dances to its own loop, and you tap along.

## The village

Between gigs, the band goes home. Resting cats busk for tips, and requests and upgrades grow the village around the stage.

## Clips

You can record a vertical clip of a gig or of a cat's dance and share it.

Dance CATharsis is free on [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=tech.hiddenproject.dancecatharsis). More screenshots are on the [game page](/dance-catharsis/).
