The Tower: Endless Idle RPG is about one question: how far can you climb? It's out now on Google Play.

<img src="/assets/tower-combat.webp" width="320" height="711" alt="A turn-based fight in The Tower on floor 78: a Rotting Tenant, with Strike, Heavy Blow, Ultimate and Interrupt." loading="lazy">

> It was already standing when you came through the door.

## A fight on every floor

Each floor is a fight. Combat is turn-based: spend energy, watch your cooldowns, and interrupt the big hit before it lands. Choose your character, learn new skills and upgrade your equipment as you go. There are gate bosses and weekly raids, too.

## Three doors

After every fight, three doors open. Each one has its own effect, and the safe door pays less. The climb runs through five biomes, from the Ashlands to the Obsidian Gate.

## Climbs while you're away

Gold piles up for hours with the game closed, and materials arrive on their own. Come back, claim it, and keep climbing.

## Falling, and climbing again

Your fall is your end. Start again from the first floor, and the marks you've earned buy ranks in Edge, Vigour and Hoard that make the next climb go further.

## Nothing to buy

No account, no premium currency, no energy timers, and no way to pay your way past a fight.

The Tower is free on [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=tech.hiddenproject.thetower). More screenshots are on the [game page](/the-tower/).
