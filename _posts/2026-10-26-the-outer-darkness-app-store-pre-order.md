The Outer Darkness is now open for pre-order on the App Store, for iPhone and iPad. With pre-registration already open on Google Play, both stores are ready for launch day.

<img src="/the-outer-darkness/shot-03.webp" width="320" height="569" alt="The Outer Darkness ship scanner, with derelicts in range around your ship." loading="lazy">

## How a pre-order works

Open [The Outer Darkness on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/the-outer-darkness-sci-fi-rpg/id6824474398) and tap **Pre-Order**. The game is free, so there's nothing to pay. On launch day it downloads to your iPhone or iPad on its own.

On Android, the same thing is [pre-registration on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=tech.hiddenproject.theouterdarkness).

## What you're signing up for

Dead ships drift back out of the dark with their holds full and their crews still aboard. Pick a derelict on your scanner, board it, aim each shot at a body part, and reach the escape shuttle with whatever you can carry. Die, and everything you brought stays aboard.

A boarding takes 5 to 15 minutes, it's played one-handed in portrait, and a run saves when you stop. There's no pay-to-win.

The [first post](/blog/the-outer-darkness-pre-registration/) walks through a boarding step by step, and the [game page](/the-outer-darkness/) has the screenshots.
