Jarlife: Merge Terrarium is a merge game set inside a glass jar. It's out now on Google Play.

<img src="/assets/jar-board.webp" width="320" height="711" alt="The jar: a grid of merged creatures during a bloom season, with seeds, eggs and fungus ready to place." loading="lazy">

## Merge to evolve

Fungus, seeds and eggs go into the jar. Drag two alike together and they merge into something new: a spore becomes moss, a microbe grows into a tiny dragon.

There are 120 creatures across Flora, Fauna and Fungi. Some come out golden, glowing, shadow or albino, and those mutations are worth hunting for.

## The jar keeps time

Seasons change, and day gives way to night. When a season ends, it pays out Seeds and lasting boosts, and the next one starts: Long Night, Monsoon, Bloom…

The jar keeps making Life while the game is closed, so there's always something new when you come back.

## The codex and the upgrades

A codex records everything that has grown in your jar, tier by tier. Upgrades change the jar itself: faster spawning, a bigger pouch, better seeds, a wider jar, a longer nap, and unstable DNA. Modifiers bend the rules from one run to the next.

Jarlife is free on [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=tech.hiddenproject.jarlife), with no sign-up. More screenshots are on the [game page](/jarlife/).
