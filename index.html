01 · Online idle RPG · Working title

The Tower

You climb a tower that does not end. Each floor is a turn-based fight, and something is already in the room when the door opens. Between fights the tower keeps working: gold and materials gather whether you are watching or not.

It was already standing when you came through the door.

Floor 78, Voidspire

Climbs pay out marks. Marks buy ranks that stay with you on the way up. Other climbers are on the same ladder, week by week.

Development build

1 / 4
Combat
Turn-based. A strike, a heavy blow, an ultimate, and an interrupt. A skill can be sealed for the turn.
Idle
Gold waits until you claim it. Materials arrive on their own.
Marks
Spent between climbs on ranks such as Edge for damage, Vigour for health, and Hoard for materials.
Ladder
Weekly and all-time floors, set against other climbers. Your best floor stays beside a haven floor.

02 · Reading log · Working title

Book Tracker

For the book that is open, not the pile you mean to start. Log a session, hold the streak, and write a short summary while the chapter is still in your head. The stats sit next to the log, so the habit is something you can see.

Progress
How far you are in the books you are actually reading.
Streak
The days in a row you log.
Summary
A short note written with each log.
Stats
The reading, counted, instead of guessed at.

03 · Merge and idle · Placeholder title

The Jar

Placeholder name. The real title is not chosen yet.

Fungus, seeds, and eggs go into a jar and merge until something new evolves. The jar keeps time: seasons change, and the day gives way to night. A codex records what you have grown. Modifiers bend the rules of a run.

Merge
Fungus, seeds, and eggs combine inside the jar.
Evolution
Merges turn into new forms.
Time
Seasons, and a day and night cycle.
Codex
What has appeared, written down.
Modifiers
Rules that change from run to run.
