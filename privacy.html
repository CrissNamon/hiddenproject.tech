Privacy

The site at hiddenproject.tech is a static page. It does not have accounts, cookies, analytics, or a server that stores what you do here. Fonts and images are part of the site itself. Opening a page does not send your visit to a third party.

If you email support@hiddenproject.tech, that message is handled like any other email you send. It is not stored by this website.
